𔓘 Soundscapes of Chettinad 𔓘

Welcome to the Saratha Vilas VR Experiment,

This application is part of a research study exploring how spatial audio shapes learning and engagement in cultural-heritage environments. Using immersive 360° visuals of Saratha Vilas, a restored Chettinad mansion in Tamil Nadu, the experiment compares different audio conditions. You will experience four versions of the walkthrough, each with a different audio setup. To keep anonymity, these are simply labelled A, B, and C, Your gaze, reaction, and responses will help us understand how sound influences cultural learning and presence.

Thank you for being part of this journey, where architecture meets audio, and your participation contributes to advancing immersive learning research.
